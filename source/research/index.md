My dissertation develops statistical methods for rare variant association tests and variant prioritization in biobank-scale data.

### VACANT: annotation-stratified rare variant association analysis

VACANT uses continuous functional annotation scores to stratify rare variants, tests their association with a trait adaptively, and estimates effect sizes for each annotation stratum. I presented it as a platform talk at IGES 2026, and earlier as posters at ASHG 2024, ASHG 2025, and the 2025 Taipei International Statistical Symposium and ICSA International Conference.

### VACANT-M: accounting for relatedness and population structure

VACANT-M is a mixed-model extension of VACANT, so that the test accounts for genetic relatedness and population structure in biobank cohorts (poster at IGES 2025).

### SHARC: recalibrating coding variant annotation scores

SHARC improves severity prediction for loss-of-function SNVs and indels, and recalibrates coding variant annotation scores across variant types for clinical variant classification (poster at ASHG 2026).

### Earlier work

- Adaptive information borrowing for the Cox model to improve prognostic prediction in triple-negative breast cancer.
- A large-scale genome-wide association study of stuttering, to which I contributed a secondary heterogeneity analysis (*Nature Genetics*, 2025).

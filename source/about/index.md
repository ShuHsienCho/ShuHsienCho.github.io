## About me

---







I am Shu-Hsien Cho (卓書賢), a Ph.D. candidate in [Biostatistics](https://gsbs.uth.edu/quantitative-sciences/) at [University of Texas MD Anderson UTHealth Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences](https://gsbs.uth.edu/), working with [Dr. Chad D. Huff](https://faculty.mdanderson.org/profiles/chad_huff.html) on statistical methods for rare-variant analysis in biobank-scale sequencing data. I expect to graduate in May 2027.

I came to statistical genetics through causal inference. After a double major in [Statistics](https://stat.nccu.edu.tw) and [Economics](https://econo.nccu.edu.tw) at [National Chengchi University](https://www.nccu.edu.tw/index.php), I worked as a research assistant with [Dr. Yen-Tsung Huang](http://www.stat.sinica.edu.tw/personinfo/?sysid=5017880) at the [Institute of Statistical Science, Academia Sinica](http://www.stat.sinica.edu.tw/statnewsite/) from 2017 to 2020, developing mediation methods for survival outcomes and analyzing clinical data on hepatitis B and liver cancer. At MD Anderson, I moved into statistical genetics and now work on rare-variant association testing and variant interpretation. See [Research](/research/) for details.

Outside research, I love baseball. I analyze baseball data and co-write a [baseball data analysis blog (in Mandarin)](https://shunyubaseball.wordpress.com) with [my friend](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yu-min-chien/?originalSubdomain=tw). I also enjoy photography, weight training, cooking, reading, wine, beer and whiskey tasting, and contract bridge.
